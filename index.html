THE CENTRAL FINDING01 / ANALYSIS

Partnership persists. So do competitive readings.

In the sources reviewed, China’s official partnership language persists through border tensions, while Chinese scholars and editorial writers continue to examine India as a strategic competitor and an autonomous power.

The diplomatic re-engagement since 2024 has not removed those competing interpretations.

Preliminary qualitative conclusion. Based on a purposive, researcher-reviewed sample—not a measured national trend. Read the limitations ↗

A NOTE ON INTERPRETATION

An evidence archive,
not a sentiment index.

Our purpose is to examine how particular institutions frame India—and where their interpretations coexist or conflict. The pilot cannot establish national opinion, representative trends or the causal effect of any diplomatic event.
